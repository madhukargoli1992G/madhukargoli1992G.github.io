ABOUT MADHUKAR GOLI

Engineering intelligent systems beyond basic LLM applications.

My work sits at the intersection of AI engineering, machine learning, information retrieval, and enterprise systems, with a focus on building production-oriented Generative AI and Agentic AI solutions for complex, data-intensive environments.

I design AI systems that go beyond basic prompt-and-response applications. My work includes Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), Agentic AI workflows, hybrid and multi-vector retrieval, semantic search, reranking, metadata-aware retrieval, validation loops, confidence scoring, and citation-grounded response generation.

I am particularly interested in solving the engineering challenges that determine whether an AI application actually works reliably in production — including retrieval accuracy, hallucination reduction, grounding, latency, token efficiency, scalability, observability, evaluation, and system reliability.

My background spans data science, analytics, machine learning, and AI systems engineering, allowing me to approach technical problems from both the data and software-engineering sides.

I work across modern AI engineering technologies including Python, FastAPI, LangGraph, LangChain, vector databases, hybrid retrieval systems, cloud platforms, containerized deployments, and modern LLM infrastructure to transform structured and unstructured enterprise data into intelligent applications.

My current direction is toward deeper AI/ML systems engineering, combining Generative AI and Agentic AI expertise with machine learning, model evaluation, scalable inference, and production AI architecture.
